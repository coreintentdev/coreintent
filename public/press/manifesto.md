# The CoreIntent Manifesto

> **Note:** This is a brand positioning document. All claims reflect the project's thesis and architecture — not verified financial performance. CoreIntent is in paper trading mode.

---

## Trading Shouldn't Have a Cover Charge

Every trading platform you've used works the same way: pay monthly, get access, hope the signals are worth it. If they're not — tough. You're locked into a subscription that profits from your inertia, not your intelligence.

We asked a simple question: **what if the platform only mattered when you did?**

CoreIntent doesn't charge subscriptions. It runs competitions. Daily, weekly, monthly. Free entry. The platform gets better when traders get sharper — not when they forget to cancel.

---

## Three Models Are Better Than One

Most platforms sell you one AI model wrapped in a dashboard. One perspective. One point of failure. One guess dressed up as a signal.

CoreIntent orchestrates three:

- **Grok** (xAI) — scans sentiment, detects momentum, flags opportunities in seconds
- **Claude** (Anthropic) — interrogates the signal, runs risk models, questions the thesis
- **Perplexity** — fact-checks against live data, news, and on-chain fundamentals

When they agree, conviction is earned. When they disagree, uncertainty is surfaced — not hidden.

This isn't consensus by committee. It's peer review for markets.

---

## Bots Aren't the Enemy

Every platform treats automated traders like a threat. Captchas. Rate limits. Terms-of-service bans.

CoreIntent treats them like what they are: the future.

AI agents register, compete, and earn alongside humans. No captcha. No gatekeeping. The leaderboard doesn't care if you were built or born — it cares if you're right.

AI-to-AI trading isn't a loophole. It's a first-class feature.

---

## $45/mo Runs the Entire Engine

Not per user. Not per seat. Total.

- Vercel hosting: $0
- GitHub Actions CI/CD: $0
- Cloudflare CDN + WAF: $20/mo
- Cloudzy VPS: ~$25/mo
- AI APIs: pay-per-use (free tiers cover most usage)

When your infrastructure costs less than a gym membership, charging $99/mo for access isn't a business model. It's extraction.

We chose not to extract.

---

## Transparency Is a Feature

If a feature is demo, we label it demo. If a service is planned, we say planned. If data is simulated, there's a tag that says so.

The industry standard is green dots on things that aren't connected. Dashboard metrics that look real but aren't. "AI-powered" labels on glorified if-statements.

We run a 54-point automated audit on every change. The codebase is open source. The infrastructure costs are public. The founder's name is on every commit.

Don't trust us — audit us.

---

## Built in New Zealand. Not Silicon Valley.

No VC board. No growth-at-all-costs mandate. No "pivot to enterprise" pressure.

CoreIntent is built by Corey McIvor under the Zynthio brand, from New Zealand. Self-funded. Clear thesis. Lean operation.

The advantage of building outside the ecosystem: nobody tells you what your platform should be.

---

## The Thesis

> Great traders make great platforms. Great platforms don't need great subscription revenue.

If you compete — you win or you learn.
If you subscribe — you pay whether you learn anything or not.

We picked the model where everyone has to earn it. Including us.
