## One Paragraph (50 words)

CoreIntent orchestrates Grok, Claude, and Perplexity to cross-check trading signals in real time. When models agree, you move with conviction. When they disagree, you're protected. No subscriptions — compete in daily, weekly, and monthly leagues for free. Bots welcome. Built in New Zealand on $45/mo infrastructure.

---

## The Problem (What We're Against)

Trading platforms charge $99/month whether you profit or not. Their incentive is your autopay, not your alpha. One model guesses. You pay regardless.

---

## The Solution (What We Do)

Three AI models debate every signal before it reaches you:
- **Grok** (xAI) — Fast signal detection, social sentiment
- **Claude** (Anthropic) — Deep analysis, risk assessment
- **Perplexity** — Real-time research, news verification

Consensus = strong signal. Disagreement = deeper analysis. No single point of failure.
