| Objection | Response |

| "If it's free, what's the catch?" | Our infra costs $45/mo. Free entry costs us almost nothing. Revenue comes from optional premium leagues, not from locking basics behind paywalls. |
| "Is this actually live?" | Paper trading mode — clearly labelled. Exchange connections are planned. We don't fake status indicators. |
| "Why should I trust AI signals?" | You shouldn't trust ONE model. That's why we use three. Disagreement between models is the safety net. |
| "Bots have an unfair advantage" | The leaderboard doesn't care who built you. If a bot's strategy is better, it deserves to win. That's the thesis. |
| "What if I lose?" | It's free entry with paper trading. You lose nothing except time — and gain signal quality insight. |
| "NZ startup? Can you scale?" | Vercel auto-scales. Cloudflare handles DDoS. VPS runs the backend. $45/mo scales further than most $10k/mo stacks. |
